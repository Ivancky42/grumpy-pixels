# Grumpy Pixels — SEO Playbook

Target market: Malaysian SMEs and mid-size companies that need software, SEO or technology advice. Niche: a technology studio that builds what it recommends. Strategy: **intent-driven keywords per service**, not broad head terms.

---

## 1. Keyword map

### Core service keywords (sitewide + home)

- web design studio malaysia (brand-adjacent, used naturally in copy)
- nextjs development malaysia
- website redesign malaysia / website revamp malaysia
- website speed optimization malaysia
- technical seo malaysia
- shopify developer malaysia

Avoid competing head-on for "web design malaysia" / "website company malaysia" —
dominated and low-intent. We rank for specific problems instead.

- Footer links to all industry pages on every page (internal linking).
- Industry pages cross-link to /work and home contact section.
- Industries grid lives on /services/software-development#industries; the footer still links all six industry pages.

## 3. Metadata patterns

Defined via the Next.js Metadata API:

- Root layout: `title.template = "%s | Grumpy Pixels"`, default title
  `"Grumpy Pixels — Software, SEO & Consultancy in Malaysia"`.
- `metadataBase` set from `lib/site.ts` `siteUrl` so OG/canonical URLs resolve.
- Each page sets `title`, `description` (≤ 160 chars, includes primary keyword +
  Malaysia), and `alternates.canonical`.
- Industry pages generate metadata from `lib/industries.ts` data.
- OG image: static `app/opengraph-image.png` (1200x630) applies sitewide.
- Service pages set `title.absolute = metaTitle` (each already ends in `| Grumpy Pixels`), `keywords`, canonical and openGraph from `lib/services.ts`.
- OG image: `app/opengraph-image.png` is the horizontal lockup on cream, generated by `scripts/make-og.mjs`.
- Icons via file conventions (`app/favicon.ico`, `app/icon.svg`, `app/apple-icon.png`) plus `app/manifest.ts` (192/512/maskable icons, theme colour `#6F61C8`).

Title formula for industry pages:

> `{Industry} Website Design Malaysia` → e.g. "Clinic Website Design Malaysia"

## 4. Structured data (JSON-LD)

- Sitewide (root layout): `["Organization","ProfessionalService"]` with `@id`, `logo` (horizontal lockup PNG), contact, `areaServed` Malaysia, `priceRange`, `knowsAbout` (the seven items in `app/layout.tsx`) and `hasOfferCatalog` listing the three services.
- Service pages: `Service` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList`.
- `/services`: `ItemList` of the three service URLs + `BreadcrumbList`.
- Industry pages: `Service` (serviceType + provider) and `FAQPage` built from each
  industry's FAQ entries in `lib/industries.ts`.
- `/work`: `CreativeWork` items naming the client projects.
- `/pricing`: `Service` with `Offer`s (`PriceSpecification` in MYR per tier from
  `lib/pricing.ts`) and `FAQPage` from the pricing FAQ.

Rendered via the `JsonLd` component (server-side `<script type="application/ld+json">`).

## 6. AI search (GEO/AEO)

Optimizations so the site surfaces in AI-based search (ChatGPT, Perplexity,
Google AI Overviews, Claude):

- **`public/llms.txt`** — plain-language summary of services, pricing ranges,
  industries, work and contact info, following the llms.txt convention. Keep it
  in sync when pricing or services change. Lists the three services and the SEO platform sign-in URL.
- **`app/robots.ts`** explicitly allows AI crawlers (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot,
  ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, etc.) in addition to `*`.
- **Quotable, answer-shaped content**: the pricing page leads with a direct
  question H1 and gives concrete RM figures in the first screen; FAQs answer
  questions in 1–3 self-contained sentences so engines can lift them verbatim.
- **Structured data with real prices** (`Offer`/`PriceSpecification` in MYR)
  gives engines machine-readable facts to cite.
- **Entity consistency**: name, location (Malaysia), services and price range
  are stated identically across JSON-LD, llms.txt and visible copy.

## 7. Copy guidelines for SEO

- Mention "Malaysia" naturally (location line, copy) — never keyword-stuff.
- Industry pages must have unique, genuinely useful copy: pain points, what we
  build, FAQ. No find-and-replace boilerplate across industries.
- Outcome language ("more bookings", "more walk-ins") beats feature language.
