# Pricing & Budget Guide Page Brief

Create a pricing page for Grumpy Pixels.

This should NOT look like a typical agency pricing page with generic Bronze, Silver and Gold packages.

The goal is to:

* Build trust
* Qualify leads
* Improve conversion rates
* Capture SEO traffic
* Provide realistic budget expectations
* Position Grumpy Pixels as a premium website studio

Tone should be:

* Honest
* Transparent
* Friendly
* Slightly playful
* Professional
* Not salesy

The page should feel like:

> "We're helping you understand website costs, not trying to sell you something."
